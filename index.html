Frequently asked

Do I need an Epic Games account to use Tracknite?

No. There is no sign-in. Just search for a public player nickname and you're in.

Why can't I see some player's stats?

Stats are shown only for public profiles. If a profile is set to private in Fortnite, its stats stay private — that's normal, not an error.

Where is my session history stored?

Entirely on your device. Nothing about your history ever leaves your phone.

What does Tracknite Premium include?

Trends and full session history, player comparison, activity heatmap and seasons, esports & Power Ranking, and the home-screen widget. It's an auto-renewing subscription with a 3-day free trial. Manage or cancel it anytime in your App Store account settings.
