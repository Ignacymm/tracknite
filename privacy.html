Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Tracknite does not track you and does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising SDKs. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

No accounts, no sign-in

You do not need an Epic Games account or any other login to use Tracknite. We never ask for a password and we never see your Epic credentials.

Public data only

Tracknite shows Fortnite statistics for public player profiles only. If a profile is set to private, its stats stay private. When you look up a player, the only thing sent to the public Fortnite data services is the nickname you searched for, in order to fetch that player's public stats, cosmetics, shop and event data.

What stays on your device

  • Session history, trends and replay analysis results are stored locally on your device (SwiftData). This data is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • App settings (theme, language, favorite skin, default tab) are stored locally on your device.
  • Notifications (e.g. a favorite skin returning to the shop) are scheduled locally on your device.

iCloud sync (your own account only)

To keep your match history and replay analysis results after you delete or reinstall the app, Tracknite can sync them to your own private iCloud database (via Apple's CloudKit) — the same private storage used by apps like Notes or Reminders. We do not have access to this data; it stays inside your Apple ID and is never sent to our servers. You can turn this off anytime in Settings → Data → "iCloud sync"; the change takes effect after restarting the app. Turning it off stops future syncing but does not delete data already synced to iCloud — you can remove that separately from your device's iCloud storage settings.

Third-party data services

To show live stats and shop items, the app requests publicly available data from third-party Fortnite APIs. These requests contain only the searched nickname and standard technical information (such as your IP address, which any internet request necessarily includes). We do not attach any identifier to these requests and we do not build a profile of you.

Subscriptions & payments

Tracknite Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled by Apple — the app never sees or stores your payment details. Purchase and subscription status is provided to the app by Apple's StoreKit. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your App Store account settings.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No personal information (name, email, address) — unless you choose to email our support.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No location data.

Children

Tracknite does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above.
